# FlashQuest launch pricing

FlashQuest launch pricing is intentionally simple:

- **Free — $0 forever**: core study, mastery scheduling, official/community decks, answer-first recall, basic Arcade, deck creation/remixing, and public Quest Rooms.
- **Pro — $7.99/month or $69/year**: advanced mastery analytics, private/invite-only rooms, larger rooms, premium study/Arcade workflows, and priority power features.
- **Educator — $19.99/month or $179/year**: everything in Pro plus cohorts, educator progress views, expanded room/deck organization, assignments/classroom workflows, and cohort analytics.

Core learning remains free. Paid checkout should not be enabled until subscription entitlements, webhook handling, cancellation, downgrade behavior, and billing UAT are complete.
